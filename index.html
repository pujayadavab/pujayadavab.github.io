Hi, I’m Puja.

My background is in business, marketing, and data analytics. I’ve also written people’s stories, helped build a writing community, and worked on research and social-impact projects. Much of what interests me now has grown out of questions I encountered while doing that work.

Lately, I’ve been thinking about how AI changes the way we create, learn, and work. I’m curious about what it makes possible, and what happens to our skills and judgment as we hand more of the work over to it.

I’m using this space to share work in progress, think through what I’m learning, and follow questions I don’t yet have answers to.

I grew up in a tiny village in the plains of southern Nepal and am currently based in the United States.

what I’m exploring now

ai&adjacent
Notes and essays on what it means to live with AI, from everyday habits to questions about creativity and learning.
Allocative Creativity
A paper exploring how creative work is divided between people and AI, and how those choices shape creativity and innovation.
The Asian Writers
A writing community I co-founded, and a place where I keep learning about what helps people make time for writing, share their work, and keep going.

along the way

Business, marketing & data
I’ve helped teams run digital campaigns, understand their audiences, and use data to decide what to do next. The work taught me to look beyond a headline number and ask what it captures and what it leaves out.
Social impact
My work has included projects in gender equity, health, education, and philanthropy. Much of what I learned came from listening to people describe their own experiences, and noticing the gaps between what a program offers and what people need.
Stories & making
I’ve interviewed people, written stories, worked on a documentary, and designed learning programs. Making things has often changed how I understand them: I notice what’s missing, rethink an assumption, or find a question I hadn’t thought to ask. That’s part of why I keep returning to creativity.
