Hi, I’m Puja.

I grew up in a tiny village in the plains of southern Nepal and am currently based in the United States. My path since then has been a little nonlinear: I’ve worked across storytelling, community-building, marketing, analytics, research, and entrepreneurship.

I’m especially interested in people, ideas, and how things get made. These days, a lot of my attention is on AI—particularly what it changes about creativity, learning, expertise, organizations, and human agency.

Outside of research, I write, build projects, and keep finding reasons to bring people together around ideas.

some things I’ve worked on

Allocative Creativity
A research model for understanding how creative activities are allocated between humans and generative AI, and what those choices mean for creativity and innovation in organizations.
ai&adjacent
An independent home for my writing and research on AI and the questions around it—creativity, organizations, human agency, expertise, and everyday life.
The Asian Writers
A writing community I co-founded, where I worked across curriculum, community-building, operations, storytelling, and experiments in giving.
AlloAI
Commercialization work for an advanced-materials startup, exploring customer needs, technical requirements, and applications for a novel aerospace alloy.
Humans of Medico
A storytelling project built from conversations with doctors, nurses, and medical students during COVID-19—an early exploration of listening, interviewing, and writing people’s lives.
AI in higher education
Work at Brandeis exploring how universities are responding to generative AI, and how emerging tools might fit into teaching and learning.
Marketing systems & analytics
I’ve worked across digital strategy, marketing analytics, experimentation, dashboards, CRM systems, funnels, and go-to-market—using data to understand how people find, choose, and engage with products.
Gender equity research
Research and programming around gender equity, including policy work, conversations with government and civil-society stakeholders, and community-based research.
Fellowships & learning communities
I’ve been part of communities that shaped how I think and work: Women Deliver Young Leaders, the GivingTuesday Starling Collective, Laconia’s VC Fellowship, and the Gograha Film Workshop—across gender equity, generosity, venture capital, filmmaking, and storytelling.
Film & storytelling
Documentary work, filmmaking training, and continuing experiments in narrative, writing, and cultural projects.

questions I’m thinking about

How should humans and AI work together?

What should we do ourselves, and what should we delegate to machines?

How does that allocation change creativity, learning, expertise, and innovation over time?

What kinds of technologies and organizations leave people more capable—not less?
